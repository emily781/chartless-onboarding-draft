Make your first note

  1. Sign inGo to app.chartlessnutrition.com and sign in with the email address you signed up with. If you have not set a password yet, use the "forgot password" link and make one now.
  2. Open your practice clientLook down the left-hand side and click Clients. There is already a practice client waiting in there for you, called Test. Click the name to open it.
  3. Give Chartless something to work fromThere are two ways to do this and neither is better than the other.

    Once you click on the client, you can either hit the Record button right from the dashboard, or you can navigate to the session notes and record from there. Then talk for two or three minutes as though a client were sitting with you. Say it out loud, roughly, the way a real visit actually sounds. It does not need to be tidy.

    The other way to create your first note is to use a session we have already written for you. Open the practice transcript, click the copy button, then in Chartless choose Add Transcript and paste it in. It is a fictional forty-five minute follow-up with labs, supplements and goals in it, so you will see what Chartless does with a whole visit rather than two minutes of talking.
  4. Tell it you have finishedClick Stop and transcribe.
  5. Wait a few minutes, then read what it wroteYour note appears under Notes on that client. Open it and read it through.
What you should see. A full clinical note in ADIME or SOAP format, written from a conversation you had two minutes ago. Alongside it you will also find a client summary, goals, and a draft Follow-up. You did not ask for any of those. They come with every session.

If the note is not quite right, that is normal and it is fixable. You can edit any part of it, and the next email is entirely about making it sound like you.

You have made a note on the practice client, so you already know what comes out the other end. This one walks you through doing it in a real visit. It is the same handful of steps, with a little more going on around them.

Record your first live session

  1. Before the visit, open the client in ChartlessClick Clients, then find them in the list or search their name. If they are not in there yet, click Add Client and enter their name. You can fill in everything else later.
  2. Ask the client if you can recordThis is the part most people worry about, and it is usually over in one sentence. Something like: "I use a tool that writes my notes so I can pay attention to you instead of my keyboard. Is it alright if I record?"

    Most people say yes, and tend to think better of you for asking. If someone says no, that is completely fine. Take notes the way you always would, paste them in afterwards, and you will get the same result.
  3. Press record, then forget about itClick Record as the visit starts and put the screen out of your mind. You do not need to watch it or manage it while it runs.

    If you do glance over, you will see the transcript appearing roughly a minute at a time. That is normal. It is not lagging behind.
  4. Type anything you want rememberedIf something occurs to you mid-visit, type it into the notes area as you go. Whatever you type is folded into the transcript alongside what was said, so it will not get lost.
  5. At the end, click Stop and transcribeThen carry on with your day. You do not need to sit and wait for it to finish.
  6. Come back and read the note throughA few minutes later it will be waiting under Notes. Read it, change anything that is not right, and [ SIGN / FINALISE - confirm the live wording ].
You are reading rather than writing. That is the whole difference, and it is worth noticing how the end of that visit feels compared with how it usually feels.

You have a real note now. If it does not sound quite like something you would have written yourself, that is expected, and it is a two-minute fix rather than something you have to live with.

Change the detail level and see the difference

  1. Open a note you have already madeGo to Clients, open a client, then Notes, and open one.
  2. Find the detail levelLook for [ WHERE THE DETAIL LEVEL SITS ]. There are four to choose from: brief, standard, detailed, and the fullest one, which works from your entire transcript rather than a summary of it.
  3. Pick a different one and run it againChoose a level you did not use the first time, regenerate, and read the two versions next to each other.
Most people are surprised by which one they prefer. Plenty of dietitians who assumed they wanted the most detailed note find they actually want the standard one, and just as many the other way round. There is no right answer here, only the one that sounds like you.

Two more things worth knowing while you are in there.

  1. You can choose a format built for your specialityGo to Settings then Note Templates. There are around thirty, covering bariatric, cardiac, eating disorder, sports medicine and others. If one suits your practice, set it and every note from then on will use it.
  2. You can keep the client version separate from the insurance versionChartless can produce a client-facing note alongside the clinical one, so what your client reads is never the same document an insurer reads. [ WHERE THIS SITS ]

Open the goals on a real client

  1. Open a client you have recordedClients, then click their name.

  3. Notice the difference between the twoA goal is something that gets finished and ticked off. A habit is ongoing, shown as a colour-coded grid so you can see the pattern at a glance.

Three more places worth a look on the same client.

  2. Growth chartsIf you see paediatric clients, these are in there as a tracked measurement. Practice Better does not have them, which is why people tend to mention it.
  3. Follow-upThe draft message to your client, already written from the session, with the goals and charts in it. It sends from your own email address, not from ours.

If you have been copying finished notes across into your EMR by hand, this is the email that gets you out of that. Take it slowly, do it with one client rather than your whole list, and it should take about fifteen minutes.

Bring one client across, and send one note back

  1. Install the extensionIn Chartless, open Account Settings and find Chartless Connect for Chrome. Click through to install it.
  2. Pin it, or you will think it is brokenThis is the step almost everybody misses. After installing, look at the top right of your browser for a small puzzle piece icon and click it. You will see a list of your extensions. Find Chartless and click the pin beside it.

    A small "C" now sits in your browser bar. Without this step there is nothing to click, and the extension looks as though it never installed at all.
  3. Log in twice, not onceClick the "C" and log in to the extension. Then open your EMR in another tab and log in there as well. Both need to be signed in before anything can move between them.

    If your password will not take, try pasting it rather than typing it. That works when typing does not.
  4. Bring one client acrossIn your EMR, open a single client. Then use the extension and choose [ YOUR EMR ] to Chartless. Start with one, so you can see exactly what arrives.
  5. Send a finished note back the other wayIn Chartless, open a note you have already finished. Switch the extension to Chartless to [ YOUR EMR ] and sync it. Then go and look at it in your EMR, so you know it landed.
  6. Leave auto-import switched off for nowThere is a setting for importing everything automatically. Leave it off to begin with, so you choose what crosses over rather than the whole list moving at once.

Hi *|FNAME|*,

This is the corner of Chartless people rarely find on their own, which is a pity, because it tends to save more time than anything else in here. There is one step that makes all the difference, and it is very easy to walk straight past.

Favourite three recipes first

  1. Open the recipe library[ WHERE THE RECIPE LIBRARY SITS ]. There are around two million recipes in there, so you will not run short.
  2. Click the heart on three recipes you already recommendNot new ones. Three you have suggested to clients before, so you can judge what comes back against something you know.
  3. Now open a client and build a planChartless uses their weight and targets to put a plan together, and it draws on the recipes you hearted.
This is why step two matters. With nothing hearted, the builder looks empty, and it is completely reasonable to assume something has broken. Nothing has. It is waiting on you to tell it what you like.

Three things worth knowing once you are in there.

  1. You can hide the numbersIf you work with eating disorders, calories and macros can be switched off for that client, and the plan goes out with none of them showing.
  2. You can filter by allergy or conditionGo gently. Stacking several filters together can narrow it down to almost nothing, which looks like a fault and is not one.
  3. You can move things aroundDrag foods in and out and the totals update as you go, so you are not recalculating anything by hand.
